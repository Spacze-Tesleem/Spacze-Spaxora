# SPACZE — Product Design

SPACZE replaces Nexora throughout the product.

## Design structure
00 — Brand Foundations: logo usage, green palette, typography, spacing, brand voice.
01 — Components: buttons, inputs, dropdowns, cards, tables, navigation, dialogs, loading states.
02 — Landing Page: introduction, interactive preview, benefits, workflow, pricing concept, signup.
03 — Authentication: signup, signin, email verification, password recovery.
04 — Onboarding: business profile, goals, audience, products, brand setup.
05 — Founder Dashboard: overview, setup progress, performance, priorities, pending approvals.
06 — Strategy: goals, customers, positioning, editable AI-assisted action plan.
07 — Products: catalogue, editor, images, pricing, inventory, descriptions.
08 — Storefront: customization, preview, product pages, cart, checkout designs.
09 — Marketing: campaign planning, content creation, leads, outreach, scheduling.
10 — Analytics: sales, traffic, conversions, campaign performance.
11 — AI Agents: responsibilities, tasks, progress, activity, permissions.
12 — Approvals: review, edit, approve, reject.
13 — Mobile: responsive founder and customer journeys.
14 — Prototype: connected core journey.

## Prototype acceptance journey
Sign up → describe business → review suggested strategy → add product → preview storefront → approve marketing draft.

## Visual direction
White and soft neutral surfaces, deep green actions, clear typography, spacious layouts. Richer animation may be used in the landing hero; daily dashboard tasks prioritize clarity. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Current milestone
Initial static landing page and four interactive sample workspace panels only. This is an initial visual draft, not the complete prototype. Authentication, onboarding, product editing, real AI, persistence, integrations, and live publishing remain unimplemented.
